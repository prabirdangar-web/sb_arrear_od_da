3. ROPA-1998 Pre-2006 Fixation (Always Frozen on 31-03-2009)

10/18/20-Year CAS Rule (ROPA-1998 phase): Regular annual increments fall on the 1st day of the Option/Fixation month as usual. 10/18/20-Year CAS dates are deferred by LWP days if entered.

4. ROPA-2009 Notional Fixation (01-04-2009 to 31-12-2019)

LWP / Notional Increment Rule (G.O. No. 181-SE(B) Dt. 8.10.2009): Regular annual increments fall on the 1st day of July (01-07-YYYY). Extraordinary Leave Without Pay on medical grounds does not defer increment. Personal LWP defers financial benefit while future 1-July cycle dates stay fixed.
